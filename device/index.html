Desk · device

Modules in their colours with two meaning knobs each — drag a module by its name strip to reorder, tap the strip to bypass it (white = bypassed). A half or black dot next to a knob = its plugin is only partly or not supported (hover for details). The master is the square XY pad; loudness on the right; MINE · BYPASS · FULL keys: hold = hear it while held, click = switch on, click again = off. Pushing a knob past its end leans the module into the bottom corner on that side. Six modules per level: more wait on the levels above and below, whose edges peek at the top and bottom of the device — ↑ ↓ on the left, Page Up / Page Down or a click on a peeking strip switch levels, drag a module up or down to move it to another level.
