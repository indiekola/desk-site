    For Ableton Live 12 with Max for Live · in development

    Turn meaning,
    not knobs.

    Desk looks at your Ableton project and sorts the tracks by what they do in the song: drums, bass, vocal, and so on. Each group gets two knobs with plain words at the ends, like tucked ↔ upfront or heavy ↔ clear. Turn a knob and Desk moves the Live devices behind it. The middle of every knob is MINE: the mix Desk set up for your track.

    01 · In your project

    Not a file. Your project.

    Desk works inside Live, on the tracks you already have. You don't export or upload anything. Press ANALYZE and Desk reads the tracks, groups them, adds knobs for the master, and sets everything up on Live's own devices.

    11tracks read

    01

    Groups by job

    Tracks are grouped by what they do in the song. The device shows six groups at a time; more are one key away.

    02

    Live's own devices

    EQ Eight, Saturator, Limiter, Utility, Drum Buss, track volume and sends. Every move happens on a device you can open, read and change.

    03

    Your devices stay

    Desk adds its own devices at the end of a track and changes values such as volume and sends. It never removes a device you put there. One Cmd+Z takes the whole analysis back.

    02 · Meaning

    Turn the meaning. Desk turns the devices.

    These are the knobs Desk made for our own track. Each goes from −10 to +10 around MINE. Point at a knob and its help text lists what it moves, in dB, Hz and %.

    03 · Your mix stays in reach

    One click back to MINE.

    MINE is the mix Desk built for your track, and the middle of every knob. BP (bypass) plays your original. Try both keys in the demo at the top.

    Mine

    Hear MINE

    Hold MINE to hear Desk's mix while you hold it, or click to switch it on and off. Grey dots remember where your own knobs were. Double-click a knob to send it back to MINE.

    Bypass

    Hear your original

    BP switches off everything Desk did, the master too. Only a safety limiter stays on. Click a group's name to switch off just that group.

    Loudness

    Loudness, measured

    A loudness meter (EBU R128, the broadcast standard) sits inside the device and reads your master while the song plays. After 10 seconds it also shows how much Spotify would turn the song up or down to reach its −14 LUFS level. This page has no audio, so here the number is an estimate from the master pad.
