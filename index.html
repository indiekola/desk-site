01 · In your project

Not a file. Your set.

Mastering services see one stereo file. Desk sits on your master and works on every track of the set: no bouncing, no uploads, no leaving Live. It groups tracks by their job in the song, and puts two knobs on each group, built on Ableton's stock devices.

01

One device on the master

Drop it on the master and sign in once. No scripts to copy, no companion app, no AI account or key of your own.

02

Modules by job, not by instrument

A rhythm guitar and a guitar solo are different jobs, so they get different tiles. Doubles ride with their source. Six modules per row; more go on rows below. Nothing is hidden.

03

Stock devices, open to you

EQ Eight, Saturator, Limiter, volume and sends. Every move Desk makes is on a device you can open, read and change.

02 · Meaning

Turn the meaning. Desk turns the devices.

The words are chosen for each project, from what the tracks can really do. These are the knobs on the promo track. Each knob reads from −10 to +10 around MINE, and Live's Info View says exactly what moved, in dB and Hz.

Info View

Device units, not a 0–100 macro

Point at a knob and Live's Info View shows each device it drives, from MINE to now: EQ Eight · Gain +1.5 dB → +2.8 dB.

Typing

Shift + digits

Focus a knob and type its value: Shift 4 = +4, Shift − 7 . 5 = −7.5, Shift 0 = MINE. Arrows step it by one; hold Shift while dragging for fine steps.

Live parameters planned

Automate it. Map it.

Every knob and both master axes become real Live parameters: draw or record automation, MIDI-map them with Cmd/Ctrl+M, and see them on Push and other controllers that follow the selected device.

03 · Your mix is the centre

MINE is always one click away.

The centre of every knob, the MINE star, is the mix Desk built for this track. BYPASS is your original. Turn away freely: comparing takes one finger. The keys drive the device at the top of the page.

Mine

Hear MINE

Hold MINE to hear Desk's mix while held; click to stay. Grey dots keep your own positions. Double-click to send every knob back to MINE.

Bypass

Hear your original

All of Desk's processing off, master included. Tap a module's name to switch just that one.

Loudness

Measured, not guessed

An EBU R128 meter inside Desk reads the master. On our promo track the loud section reads −13.3 LUFS short-term at MINE, true peak −1.01 dBTP. Streaming shows what normalisation will do (reference −14 LUFS).

04 · Honest

When it can't, it says why.

Desk drops a meaning it can't deliver honestly instead of faking it. Three from our promo track:

Pad · width

No device on these tracks controls width, and Desk won't fake it with pan.

Not offered
Drums · space

Reverb and delay sends are already off: there is no drier side to turn toward.

Not offered
Vocal · harshness

No EQ band on the vocals works in the harsh range, and the nearest one already belongs to tucked ↔ upfront.

05 · What you need

One device. Nothing else to install.

Desk is in development; this describes the planned release.

Live

Ableton Live 12 with Suite, or Live 12 Standard with the Max for Live add-on.

Setup

One device on the master, one sign-in. No AI key, no scripts.

Offline planned

Once built, knobs, MINE and BYPASS work without a connection; their state is saved in your set.

Your audio

Stays on your machine. Track names, device names and measurements go to our service; audio only if you allow it for one request.

Your theme planned

Desk follows Live's theme. Desk look keeps its colours; Live look takes your track colours.

Help planned

No pop-ups: every control explains itself in Live's Info View.
